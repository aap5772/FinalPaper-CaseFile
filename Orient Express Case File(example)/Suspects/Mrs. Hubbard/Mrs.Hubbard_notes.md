Mrs. Hubbard
“There was a man in my compartment!”
“I locked that door—I am sure of it.”
“I am a nervous woman, Monsieur Poirot.”

Investigator Notes

Highly emotional; testimony is dramatic and emphatic.
Repeats key claims (intruder, locked door) insistently.
Behavior may exaggerate fear to reinforce credibility.

Suspicious Detail

The claim of a man entering her compartment conflicts with physical constraints and other testimonies.
