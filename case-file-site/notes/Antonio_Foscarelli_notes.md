Antonio Foscarelli
“I have been Mr. Ratchett’s valet for some months, sir.”
“I heard nothing in the night.”
“I suffer from no nerves, Monsieur Poirot.”
“I took him his bromide, as usual.”

Investigator Notes

Speech is controlled, flat, and unusually formal for a servant under questioning.
Emphasizes routine (“as usual”) repeatedly, suggesting rehearsed testimony.
Lack of emotional reaction to employer’s death is notable.

Suspicious Detail

Claims to have heard nothing despite proximity and light sleeping conditions typical of valets.
